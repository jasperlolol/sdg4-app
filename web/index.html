Real progress, but nowhere near enough

Better than in 2000

  • +327 millionmore students in primary and secondary school (2000 to 2024)
  • 37% → 61%of young people complete upper secondary school
  • 87% → 93%of young people can read and write
  • 25% → 18%of girls are married before age 18 (2000 to 2025)

Still off track

  • 273 millionout of school in 2024, up for the seventh year in a row
  • 58 in 100students reach the minimum reading level by the end of primary school (2019)
  • 7.5%of development aid went to education in 2024, the lowest share in two decades
  • 2105is when 95% of young people would finish upper secondary school at today’s pace

Sources: UNESCO GEM Report 2026; UN SDG Report 2026; UNESCO GEM Gender Report 2026. Details on the About page.
