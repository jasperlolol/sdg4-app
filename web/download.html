Get the app

Take SDG 4 with you

Install the site as an app on your computer or phone. Each app opens the full website and keeps working without internet once it has been opened.

Windows

Windows 10 and 11

Download the installer and run it. Windows may say the publisher is unknown. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Download for Windows

Mac

Mac, Apple or Intel chip

Open the downloaded file and drag SDG 4 into Applications. The first time, macOS may block it. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

Download for Mac

Android

Phones and tablets

Open the downloaded file. Android will ask you to allow installs from this source. Turn that on, then install.

Download for Android

iPhone and iPad

Add it to your Home Screen

There is no download for iPhone and iPad. Open the website in Safari, tap the Share button, then tap Add to Home Screen.

Open the website

The apps are built automatically and published on GitHub, where older versions are also kept. The apps are not signed by Apple, Microsoft or Google, so the warnings above are expected.
