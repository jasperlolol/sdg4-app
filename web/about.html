What this is

This website was made for a school presentation on SDG 4, one of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals that all UN member states agreed in 2015. It brings together the latest data, the ten targets, what has worked and what students can do.

It is not an official United Nations or UNESCO website. The data were compiled on 28 October 2026 from the reports listed under Sources. When a number has a year next to it, that is the year the data describe.

Glossary

The terms the reports use, in plain words.

Completion rate
The share of young people who have finished a level of school, counted among those three to five years older than the age for its last grade. It shows how many actually finish, not just start.
Out-of-school rate
The share of children or young people of the official age for a level of school who are not enrolled in any school.
Enrolment
Being registered at a school or college. Students who are enrolled can still miss lessons or leave before they finish.
Gross enrolment ratio
Everyone enrolled at a level, whatever their age, divided by the number of people of the official age for that level. It can go above 100% when older or younger students are enrolled.
Minimum proficiency level
The basic level of reading or maths a student should reach by a stage of school, such as the end of primary school. It is measured with learning tests.
Parity index
A way to compare two groups, such as girls and boys or the poorest and the richest. A value of 1, or 100 for every 100, means both groups do equally well.
Pre-primary education
Organised learning for young children before primary school, usually from about age 3.
Primary education
The first stage of formal school, usually about six years long and starting at around age 6.
Lower secondary education
The stage after primary school, usually about three years long.
Upper secondary education
The last stage of school before university, training or work, usually ending at around age 17 or 18.
Tertiary education
Education after secondary school, such as university, college and advanced vocational training.
Literacy
Being able to read and write, with understanding, a short simple statement about everyday life.
Qualified teacher
A teacher who has at least the minimum qualifications their country requires to teach at that level.
Official development assistance (aid)
Money from governments to support development in lower-income countries, given as grants or as loans on generous terms.
SDGs
The 17 Sustainable Development Goals that all UN member states agreed in 2015 to reach by 2030. Education is Goal 4.
MDGs
The 8 Millennium Development Goals, set in 2000 for 2015. MDG 2 aimed for every child to finish primary school.
UNESCO
The United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization. It leads the global work on SDG 4.
GEM Report
The Global Education Monitoring Report, an independent report published by UNESCO every year that tracks progress on SDG 4.

No terms match. Try a shorter word, such as “school”.

How it was made

The site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no outside libraries, fonts or trackers, so it also works offline. The sound effects are made in the browser with the Web Audio API. Quiz scores, timer settings and your pledge are saved only in your own browser.
