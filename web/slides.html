Sustainable Development Goal 4

Quality education for all

“Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all.”

Agreed by all 193 UN member states in 2015. Deadline: the end of 2030.

Access

273 million

children and young people were out of school in 2024. That is 1 in 6, and the number has risen seven years in a row.

Source: UNESCO GEM Report 2026

Who is missing

130 million

of them are of upper-secondary age. Another 79 million are of primary-school age and 64 million of lower-secondary age.

79Mprimary age64Mlower secondary130Mupper secondary

Source: UNESCO GEM Report 2026, Chapter 7

Finishing school

61%

of young people worldwide finish upper secondary school. In sub-Saharan Africa, it is 28%.

Source: UN SDG Report 2026 (2024 data)

Learning

58 in 100

students reach the minimum reading level by the end of primary school. In maths, it is 44 in 100.

Source: UN SDG Report 2026 (latest global estimate, 2019)

Teachers

44 million

more teachers are needed by 2030, 15 million of them in sub-Saharan Africa. Today, 1 in 5 teachers lacks the minimum qualifications.

Sources: UNESCO Global Report on Teachers 2024; UN SDG Report 2026

Money

$97 billion

is missing every year for low- and lower-middle-income countries to reach their own education targets.

113countries spend more on debt repayments than on education

What works

Four things with evidence behind them

+1 yearof schooling when 14 African countries made school compulsory, not just free
36%more likely to be enrolled when families get cash tied to school attendance
Half a yearof extra learning for every US$100 spent on school meals
Nearly 1 yearof extra schooling linked to electrification in Cambodia

Source: UNESCO GEM Report 2026

The deadline

2105

At today’s pace, that is when 95% of young people would finish upper secondary school. The deadline is the end of 2030, 1,525 days from now.

Source: UNESCO GEM Report 2026

What we can do

Three things any student can do

  • Learn the facts and share themAlways with their source.
  • Help the people around youTutor younger students, include classmates and speak up about bullying.
  • Ask leaders to fund educationSchools, teachers and school meals all depend on it.
