                Classrooms and privacy

                Classrooms need an internet connection. A teacher can see a student's nickname, avatar, bio, quiz scores and when they last took a quiz. Classmates can see the nickname, avatar, colour and bio. Nothing else is shared.

                Erasing your data on this device also forgets your teacher account here, so classrooms you made on this device can no longer be managed.
